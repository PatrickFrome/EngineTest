# Architecture — METAENGINE 16X 2.0

## Principle

The problem is allowed to reorganize the computation. All sixteen lineages see the source in the diagnostic wave; only deep execution is sparse. A deep pass is scheduled because it is expected to change the problem-space, not because an engine has a permanent rank.

## Layers

1. **Diagnostic 16-way primary** — capability-preserving baseline and immutable native outputs.
2. **Typed 240-bridge mesh** — direct typed reuse where contracts match, provenance-preserving context projection elsewhere.
3. **Claim + disagreement state** — truth-bearing claims remain separate from derived generative state.
4. **Expected-gain scheduler** — contextual priors × tension × independence × pair synergy × cost.
5. **Temporary coalitions** — hermeneutic, evidence, graph, memory, workflow, hypothesis or adversarial groups assembled per problem.
6. **Topology evolution** — fixed library topologies plus generated gain/disagreement candidates.
7. **Native re-entry compiler** — converts derived pressure into engine-native work without turning derived pressure into evidence.
8. **Transformation Graph** — causal topology of how one operation changes the space of another.
9. **Depth budget** — stops on recursive echo, low marginal gain, proliferation, hard safety budget or max-depth safety.
10. **16-way cross-review + arbitration + fusion without erasure**.

## Transformation Graph semantics

The graph can contain nodes such as `QUESTION`, `SEGMENTATION`, `DESTRUCTION`, `INTERPRETATION`, `SEMANTIC_DIFFERENTIAL`, `OPERATOR`, `PARSE`, `EVIDENCE_REQUEST`, `CONTRADICTION`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `MEMORY`, `WORKFLOW`, `COUNTERFACTUAL`, `ABSTENTION`, `REGROUNDING`, `ARCHITECTURE_TOPOLOGY` and `COALITION`.

Edges such as `CHANGES_SPACE_OF`, `REENTERS_AFTER`, `MUTATES_TOPOLOGY`, `SCHEDULES`, `TRANSFORMS` and `REGROUND_REQUIRED` have `truth_effect = NONE`. Causal depth therefore measures reorganization, not correctness.

## Sparse deep execution

The 16-way diagnostic wave is invariant. Deep execution may follow shapes such as `16 diagnostic → 4 deep → 3 deep → 2 deep → stop`, or another topology induced by task and history. The scheduler cannot promote claims.

## Engine biography semantics

Biographies record domain-conditioned marginal gain, cost, pair synergy, failure modes and topology history. Low sample counts are shrunk toward a neutral 0.5 prior. An unobserved engine is **unknown**, not weak.

## Architecture mutation lifecycle

A topology candidate can be `RETAIN_FOR_TASK_CLASS`, `RETIRE_ARCHITECTURE_NO_MARGINAL_TRANSFER` or `QUARANTINE_ARCHITECTURE_FALSE_CONFIDENCE`. Persistent disagreement may itself trigger a topology mutation.

## Provenance firewall

Every deep dossier contains an explicit `ORIGINAL_SOURCE` section and a separate `DERIVED PRESSURES` section. Re-entry outputs are `GENERATIVE_ONLY` until source-regrounded. Native claim nodes and positions are rebuilt only from primary contributions.
