# Destruktion 4.0 — Project Development Report 0.10

## Revision thesis

Version `0.10.0-alpha.1` implements the two limits left open by 0.9: **independent operator-family birth** and **micro-local downstream integration**. The method no longer assumes that every resistant source should be handled by a variant of relation-genesis, and a micro-local ecology no longer has to be collapsed back into one corpus-level living/expert thesis.

The new loop is:

`source resistance → interrogative-family birth → local multi-family ecology → boundary/residual preservation → downstream local adjudication → global synthesis or abstention`.

No family birth, local selection, composition or downstream result promotes an operator into frozen CORE.

## Independent processual-hermeneutic family

A second experimental family, `PROCESSUAL_HERMENEUTIC_PROFILE`, is implemented independently of `RELATION_GENESIS_PROFILE`. It can be source-forced by recurrent temporal, enactment, practice, material-mediation, absence/trace and address/response patterns. Its initial local profiles are `TEMPORAL_ENACTMENT`, `PRACTICE_MEDIATION`, `ABSENCE_DISCLOSURE` and `RESPONSIVE_ENACTMENT`.

The living layer receives a separate processual interrogative lens. It distinguishes chronology from temporalization, report from enactment, practice from represented content, mediation from transmission, absence from non-existence, trace from causal proof, and response from passive reception. The lens also self-criticizes its own risk of turning process language into a universal metaphysics.

## Source-birth benchmark ceiling

Three small origin DOCXs were created as **controlled synthetic developer regression corpora** for temporal enactment, practice/material mediation and absence/trace. They are explicitly not primary philosophical sources and cannot validate an author, historical genealogy or ontology. Each real refinery/living run nevertheless had to produce the processual family and its source-forced GX7 method mutation before the candidate could enter competition.

All three source-birth audits passed.

## Independent-family micro-local regression

A six-window controlled benchmark then placed processual and relation-genesis regimes inside one document:

| Window | Expected/observed routing |
|---|---|
| W1 Temporal enactment | `TEMPORAL_ENACTMENT_SOURCE_BORN` |
| W2 Practice/material mediation | `PRACTICE_MEDIATION_SOURCE_BORN` |
| W3 Absence/trace | `ABSENCE_DISCLOSURE_SOURCE_BORN` |
| W4 Geviert-style relation control | `GEVIERT_CO_CONSTITUTIVE_GATHERING` |
| W5 Cross-family composition | relation + temporal processual composition |
| W6 Descriptive negative control | `ABSTAIN_UNRESOLVED` |

The run passed `6/6` preregistered window expectations. W5 is the first implemented composition between **two independently born operator families** rather than two profiles of one family. W6 keeps the whole ecology from forcing an operator onto a direct descriptive passage.

The higher-level synthesis remains `PRESERVE_POLYPHONIC_LOCALITY`.

## Downstream preservation

The new `ecology-downstream` executor consumes the micro-local result and constructs a provenance-preserving living graph plus local expert adjudications. The current run preserves six local windows, one open boundary, one local residual and two independently born operator families. It returns `POLYPHONIC_GLOBAL_ABSTENTION` with `thesis_allowed=false` rather than manufacturing a single global thesis.

Local statuses distinguish qualified single-family routing, qualified cross-family composition and insufficient local routing.

## Bug discovered by the new family

The first processual detector produced a false `ADDRESS_RESPONSE` activation because the cue `call` matched the substring in the word `locally`. This would have inflated process evidence without a source-local lexical occurrence. The process detector was therefore changed to Unicode letter/number lexical boundaries. The negative-control window was also rewritten so that it contains no hidden operator-trigger vocabulary.

This is methodologically important: a supposed increase in hermeneutic sensitivity was partially an artefact of token matching, and the benchmark forced the project to expose and repair it.

## Epistemic nonlinearity added in 0.10

The project can now distinguish at least these cases:

1. a source forces a relation-genesis family;
2. a source forces a processual-hermeneutic family;
3. different families win in different windows;
4. two families add non-redundant local distinctions and compose without global fusion;
5. a window activates none and the method abstains;
6. local polyphony survives into downstream living/expert representations;
7. global thesis formation can be refused after downstream integration.

This is a stronger form of nonlinearity than branch multiplication: the *kind of question asked of a passage* can change locally, and several kinds of questioning can remain irreducible.

## Remaining risks

- processual signal families are still engineered from a finite cue vocabulary;
- synthetic origin corpora validate routing mechanics only, not philosophical adequacy;
- local profile scoring remains heuristic rather than an externally measured semantic loss function;
- the downstream layer is currently an adapter beside the existing living/expert cycles rather than their native common substrate;
- the project can still overfit source wording and mistake recurrent vocabulary for a genuinely new interrogative family;
- `PROCESSUAL_HERMENEUTIC_PROFILE` could become a new totalizing language of process, event, absence and enactment;
- blinded external domain-expert semantic validation remains pending.

## Strongest next stage

The next qualitative step should be **dynamic interrogative-family induction plus adversarial external validation**. Instead of predeclaring the next family vocabulary, the system should propose candidate distinctions from source-native patterns, compare them against existing families, and require blind expert review on real corpora before a new family is retained. In parallel, micro-local ecology should become the native substrate consumed directly by both living and expert cycles rather than a post-hoc downstream adapter.

The key question becomes:

`can a source force a genuinely new way of asking, while the system can still distinguish novelty from lexical noise and refuse to canonize its own invention?`

## Release ceiling

`0.10.0-alpha.1` remains a developer-tested research alpha. The 0.10 benchmarks establish internal mechanics of family birth, local multi-family routing and downstream preservation only. They do not establish philosophical truth, authorial fidelity, external semantic validity or CORE promotion. `CORE 4.0.0-alpha.1` remains frozen.
