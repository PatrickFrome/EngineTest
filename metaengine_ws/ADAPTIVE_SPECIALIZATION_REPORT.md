## Architectural implication

The result suggests a move from fixed orchestration toward an empirical **specialization memory**:

- preserve universal participation for the initial mixed rounds;
- record the marginal novelty and later adjudicated utility of each architecture by task class;
- route later recursive budget according to observed marginal contribution;
- never convert frequency into a truth weight;
- retain minority/challenger roles even for engines with low average frequency when they uniquely expose failure modes.

Claim ceiling: `EARLY_CONTROLLED_DYNAMIC_SPECIALIZATION_SIGNAL_NOT_GENERAL_RELIABILITY_RANKING`.
